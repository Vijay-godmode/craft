THE STANDARD

What this builder deliberately avoids

  • Invented skills, education, employers, certificates, or experience
  • Hidden text, keyword stuffing, columns, icons, and tables in the exported resume
  • LinkedIn scraping or applying without your explicit approval
  • Claiming a score predicts a company's hiring decision or a compensation package
